## Their system structure (for reference — don't rebuild, this is theirs)
Project instructions load, in order: `Claude role.md` → `SEO & GEO Goals H1 2026.md` → `Brand overview.md` → `TIB_Customer_Persona.md` + `Big_Sellers_Persona_Buying_Journey.md` → `All Teeinblue feature.md`. They have 3 skills: `/tib-serp-analysis-then-outline-creation`, `/tib-serp-content-gap`, `/tib-content-review`. Mission: rank top-10 on Google/Bing AND be cited/recommended by AI engines (ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini).

## Canonical brand voice (use this exactly — don't improvise a different one)
- **Brand characteristics**: Friendly, Passionate, Professional, Proactive, Authentic.
- **Brand voice**: Intimate & caring · Enthusiastic & eager to help · Informative/clear/fair (not overly emotional, biased, or wordy) · Thoughtful & determined.
- Treat the reader as **a smart business owner** — lead with clear guidelines, outcomes, and proof points, not general descriptions.
